# V24-B prospective confirmation of active routing
V24-A training and validation yielded zero differences between branch outcomes; regression chose the high branch throughout development. The authors saw A's development record but no private held-out outcomes or estimates when specifying B. Preserve A, identify it as a negative routing control and publish its evaluation. B is not a rerun on A test data. All simulation, test and development worlds and five bank pairs of B are new, and sample size, underlying physical distribution and calibration menu are unchanged. Primary claim about active routing rests on B only; do not combine A/B samples or choose between them based on performance.
Keep A endpoints, calibration-specific margins, timing, bootstrap procedure and static/forced/random controls. Same factual host-side ridge; new continuous training target is log(1e-7 + RMS of centered low-budget minus K2048 action risks), computed from public simulator bank, no true label needed for that target.
