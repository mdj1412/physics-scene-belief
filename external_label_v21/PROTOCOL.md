# V21 frozen design
5 independent randomized Sobol inner/outer bank pairs, N=16384 each. Common acquisition plan and representative quantiles across pairs to isolate bank variation. 512 fresh held-out worlds x 16 shared actual observation noise repeats, paired across nominal and two deliberately withheld flexible contact laws. New laws affect flexible class only; same base classes and matched randomness provide a negative control. Nominal inference for every cell. Law 1 uses a speed-weakening friction coefficient with a Coulomb threshold and exact sticking; law 2 uses direction-dependent smooth tangential dissipation. Laws specified in data_v21.py before run. Pilot and development only nominal, disjoint world seeds. Shared actual decoder per bank for all methods (full inner bank). Primary target: population over fresh worlds and bank pairs, conditional on the three fixed laws and acquisition plan. Equal weights for the two unseen laws; no claim about a population of all laws.
Replicate full V20 2x2x2 at M512, average realized loss across both Y laws and both label modes then compare full E vs C label source. Cheap policy: Y from external full bank, fixed compact prediction q (2*256), conditional Brier loss using external labels from top-K hypotheses by factual posterior weight; keep original log weights in each action update. Top K candidates 128,512,2048 at M512,2048; fixed global K128/M4096 unchanged. Baselines always-probe, 8 cells; frozen chosen candidate only on test. Numeric check against untruncated dense external conditional mean for K=N, and full actual probabilities. Pilot on separate bank pair using first 24 development worlds and synchronized batch1 (3 shuffled timed passes after warmup), common p90 latency cap = same-pass fixed global p90. Choose eligible candidate with lowest development Brier over 128 development worlds (tie by measured p90). If none fits choose fastest, declare cap failure. No test-based changes.
Primary 4 comparisons: E-C label source on nominal, E-C equal-weighted two unseen laws; selected cheap minus fixed global on nominal, same on unseen mean. Negative supports gain. Two-way crossed paired bootstrap (resample bank pairs and worlds independently, preserve laws/methods/noise pairing), 20000 draws, Bonferroni 98.75% intervals; secondary 95% by law and seed signs, leave-one-bank means. Bank count small; also conditional world-only intervals and per-law per-bank results. Claim stronger compute/accuracy only if cheap contrasts upper bounds <0 for nominal and unseen plus measured test pooled GPU p90 <= fixed global in both. Report per-law p50/p90 and per-seed p90 on first 16 test worlds per law/pair x2 repeats, randomized interleaving with warmup. Include selection, topk, factual fits and actual observation update; exclude static bank setup and sensing. Capture policy choices before reading test private outcomes. Conditional label source full vs cheap vs compact at matched E-Y/mean and M512 is a secondary mechanism check. Conditional lost factual mass alone cannot bound posterior after seeing Y.
