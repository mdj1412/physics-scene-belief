# v69 server comparison plan

The Bristol author CSV is fixed. Entire campaigns A→B and B→A are held out in turn. Each source uses contiguous blocks of 24, with zero-based block index mod 4 = 1 reserved for uncertainty calibration. The other blocks fit shared normal restitution by normal-velocity least squares and tangential parameters by equally weighted squared post-impact COM and rim velocity error. No test targets determine a parameter. Camera-reported positive anticlockwise rotation gives contact speed u = vx + R omega; R = 0.02135 m (nominal). Velocities and rim speeds are in m/s.
 
Main mechanical contrast: real PyBullet DIRECT against a sphere-adapted v68 asymmetric normal spring with Coulomb impulse. Physics beyond baseline: use Coulomb-limited tangential spring memory and calibrate stiffness ratio on the same source split. The flat surface with identical moment of inertia and Coulomb predicts equality of the first two models in the small-step limit; report contrasts even if zero. No in-contact stick/slip, pressure, contact duration or identity can be read from these CSVs. Event primary = absolute terminal rigid-contact speed <=0.5 m/s; sensitivity .25 and 1 m/s, plus passage through zero from a sign reversal. The uncertain probability comes from the source held-back signed terminal residual distribution. Export every solver prediction and source split, fit parameters and numerical checks. Actual Bullet validation is against an independent rigid impulse on all trials, not a replacement for a Bullet run. Evaluate MAE/RMSE in both tangential COM and contact point velocities, Brier and prevalence. Use the idle allocated GPU for compliant integration and scoring; PyBullet DIRECT executes on CPU of the allocation.
