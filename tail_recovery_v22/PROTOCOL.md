# V22 prospective protocol
Five new independent Sobol inner/outer bank pairs, 512 fresh held-out worlds, three paired laws (nominal, speed_threshold, directional_damping); common 16 intervention noise draws per action/world. Stick Brier with same full inner decoder. Inference nominal. Primary comparison uses common GPU latency (batch 1 synchronized p90 with fixed global, all online computation including selection and chosen update). Matched mechanistic comparison separately at fixed K=512,M=512: original top, tail-preserving, full external label posterior. Deployment M=2048 for top and preserving, K independently chosen via development timing only. Fixed global original kernel K128/M4096. Always probe descriptive.
Preserver: keep largest 3K/4 factual hypotheses exactly; split remaining support by public candidate query label; within each label inverse-CDF stratified resample K/8 particles with replacement, independently seeded single uniform per stratum (seed and plan fixed in deployment), assign group mass divided by slot count. Zero mass handled. Exact head weights, tail label masses conserved, duplicates legitimately count as slots. The tail beyond the operational diagnostic top-K is a subset of this resampled tail. No actual observation used. Include fixed K mechanism choices plus common-M full oracle-of-compression (finite posterior expectation) and matched deployment top/keep/fixed.
Calibration: distinct nominal 32-world pilot and independent bank. K from 256 to 5376 step 256 for both at M2048, warm, randomized blocks over 16 worlds x2; then independent second 16 worlds x3 on nearest 4 candidates of each family, choose pair minimizing worst absolute log-ratio to fixed and to each other, target cap at fixed with fallback to closest pair. GPU near-parity range 0.90-1.10 vs fixed and each other on held-out measurements, report ratios even on failure. No sleep padding. 5 banks x first 24 held-out worlds per law x 2 randomized repeats; no test-based selection.
Recovery diagnostics for reference K512 and chosen top K using full external factual and actual action posterior. Tail group = hypotheses outside factual top K. Material recovery = factual mass <=5%, posterior tail mass >=10%, >=5x growth. Also individual new entrant = excluded hypothesis with post weight >=1% and >=10x factual weight. Record top excluded posterior atom, factual rank and fold-change, recovered collective mass, actual event-probability error, predictive surprise (-log whitened marginal density up to action constant). By action, law, surprise within action, and fixed vs matched budget. For risk attribution compare common full/ top/ keep risks and choices at same K/M; post hoc any material recovery over the first 8 noise draws for any action defines subgroup; evaluate paired Brier on held-back last 8 to avoid selecting on evaluation repeats. Every actual per-action update only after test choices frozen. Actual observation cannot cause an earlier action; full-vs-truncated computational intervention identifies the selector contrast; realized mass subgroup is diagnostic, not an online signal or causal estimate.
Primary four comparisons: preserving minus top and fixed on nominal and equal-weight two unseen laws. Paired two-way world/bank bootstrap 20000 draws; Bonferroni 98.75% two-sided CI. Claim lower loss only if both baselines beaten and latency matched. Secondary 95% intervals descriptive. Report action agreement, risk gaps, conditional Brier delta, conditional change vs all-world average; base-class negative control, mass and probability identity checks. Correlated noise/actions/laws are not extra independent samples.
